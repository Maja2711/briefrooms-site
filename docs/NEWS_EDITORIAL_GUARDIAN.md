# BRs News Editorial Guardian — v1

**Authority:** CT-01 / IN-01 only. The canonical news publisher is the sole writer. The independent hourly health audit owns observation, incident reporting and bounded recovery. Engineering Harness review has no deployment or trading authority.

## Contract

- Before ranking, scan qualified source and carry candidates for previews replaced by a later reported interview. Match explicit named guest, programme, time order, attributed speech and renderable source evidence. Remove the preview, not the report. Keep preview if a matching report is absent or unqualified.
- Compare cards globally across sections and separately across homepage plus reserve. Intentional reuse of the same article on the homepage and its own desk is allowed. A preview in reserve is obsolete even if the report is available only in a desk.
- Confirm identical source URL/canonical event, known fact signatures or a high-precision independently anchored paraphrase. Preserve distinct material developments, dates, guests and programmes. Do not equate the same photo, speaker or broad topic with the same event.
- Publish the remaining qualified set even when underfilled; no timestamp reset, stale padding, publisher quota relaxation or synthetic content. Use existing ranking and reserve mechanism. Corrections are bounded to selection; no background rewrite or direct feed mutation by the watchdog.
- Health audit rescans the actual feed, reports pair findings and marker to data/system/news_live_health.json, and uses the existing incident and recovery contract. No new autonomous writer or retry loop.

## Regression and limitations

The 27 November 2026 Polsat News Graffiti preview and later Motyka report are regression fixtures. Test English and Polish, different time windows/programmes/persons, independent developments, missing evidence, reserve and cross-desk duplication. Existing source/event/claim/freshness and image requirements remain authoritative. Version 1 is deterministic, conservative and does not claim general language-model semantic completeness. Unknown or ambiguous paraphrases may survive; use the Harness reviewer to propose audited regressions, never automatic code promotion.

## Operational lifecycle

Publisher admission -> editorial guard -> canonical selection and existing home ranking -> freshness and render -> hourly independent health audit -> existing incident/recovery. A missing report is not an incident. No change to urgent rail, permanent archives, Belief, trading or forecast state. Rollback by reverting the reviewed PR.
