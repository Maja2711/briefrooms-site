Model objective and rules

Maximise capital value over roughly five years with moderately high risk, without leverage or CFDs.

  1. No leverage and no CFDs
  2. Weekly review of price trend, drawdown, volatility, earnings calendar and material news
  3. Target weights are guides, not automatic trading instructions
  4. A single stock should normally remain below 18% of portfolio value
  5. A broad ETF may remain up to 30% of portfolio value
  6. Rebalancing threshold: more than 5 percentage points away from target or more than 1.5 times target weight
  7. Closed history and weekly snapshots are append-only

Model portfolio and educational material — not investment advice.
