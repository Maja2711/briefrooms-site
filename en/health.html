How BriefRooms Health is built

The goal is to surface practical information quickly without hiding uncertainty or limitations.

Sources

Priority goes to medical-society guidelines, public-health institutions, systematic reviews and peer-reviewed research.

Tools

Calculators are educational. Where possible, calculations run locally in the browser and do not require health data to be sent to the server.

Limitations

Content is not a diagnosis or personalised medical advice. Important health decisions should be discussed with a qualified professional.
