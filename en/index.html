Birth of pine marten kits 'a fantastic sign'

Pine marten kits have been born on Exmoor for the first time in over a century, as shown by new footage. The Two Moors Pine Marten Project introduced nine females and ten males from the Scottish Highlands to the area last year. Conservationists are excited about the successful breeding, but they caution that the pine marten's return to the region is still fragile. The project has involved local community efforts, including building den boxes and monitoring the animals.
