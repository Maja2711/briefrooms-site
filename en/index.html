Knowledge · analysis · decision

BriefRooms is a knowledge and decision platform.

We combine credible sources, explicit uncertainty modelling and artificial intelligence in an auditable process: from information, through the state of knowledge, to decisions and later evaluation of their outcomes.

We are not building an “AI text farm”.

A language model is a tool, not a source of truth. The direction for BriefRooms is a process in which narrative comes after evidence has been collected and structured, while decisions can later be confronted with real outcomes.

About the project

BriefRooms is an evolving research-and-product project. We do not assume every experimental layer will prove useful — results, auditability and repeatability have to justify it.
